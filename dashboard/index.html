Validation boundaries

The safeguards that protect the interpretation of the results

01Dates do not cross splits

Films fabricated on the same date stay together in the corresponding train, validation or test partitions.

02Fit transformations on training data

Spectral scaling and target normalization are learned from the training partition within each fold.

03Independent spectral budgets

Each 16–351-channel CNN is fitted on only the observed channels for that configuration.

04No target-derived optical features

Thickness and any spectral descriptors computed using thickness are excluded from the model input.

UNCERTAINTY

What the bootstrap means

10,000 resamples of the 20 fabrication-date groups produce a descriptive 95% percentile interval for CNN MAE. It is not a calibrated guarantee for new labs, instruments or materials.

EXTERNAL VALIDITY

What remains untested

No independent laboratory, new optical instrument, cross-composition dataset, or prospective deployment has been validated. The original Mendeley study is still pending.
